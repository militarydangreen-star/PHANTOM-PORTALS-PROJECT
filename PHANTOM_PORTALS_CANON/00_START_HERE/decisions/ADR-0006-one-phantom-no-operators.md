# ADR-0006: One hooded Phantom; operator personas and energies stay retired

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-29
- **Canon version:** 1.0.0

## Context
The Operator System gave the product seven operator identities, each with its own energy colour (green, cyan, red-pink, orange, violet, teal, pink). The current spec says: "Use one recognizable hooded Phantom identity, not competing characters or operator personas." The key visual is nonetheless titled *Plate 01 — The Operator*.

## Decision
- The Phantom is the only character: one per surface, never repeated across panels, never recoloured, never joined by a second figure.
- No operator personas, operator colours, operator themes or operator matrix.
- *The Operator* stays as Plate 01's title because it names the Phantom's role in the field-manual fiction, not a persona system.

## Consequences
- Module identity comes from icons, labels and module IDs (`MOD-04`), never from per-module colour.
- Prompts include "a second character" and "operator colour themes" in their negative lists.
