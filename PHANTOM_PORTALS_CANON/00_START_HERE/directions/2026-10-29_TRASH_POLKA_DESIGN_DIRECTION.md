# Phantom Portals — white-forward Trash Polka standard

**Owner direction:** October 29, 2026. **Scope:** every Phantom Portals and PHANTOM USB designed surface. This is the current visual authority and supersedes the October 17 Cyberpunk direction and older Forest, Ruby, Blood Badger, and alpine looks wherever they conflict. Existing behavior, data, boot invariants, launch paths, and unrelated edits remain in place while their presentation changes.

## Working visual system

The approved v3.1 white-forward Trash Polka × isometric prototype is the reference. Use substantial white or pale paper reading planes, matte black application chrome and terminal planes, blood crimson interaction accents, energetic ink and brushwork, and precise isometric structure. The hooded Phantom is the hero character. Avoid making every card grungy; keep long-form workspaces quiet and readable.

The reference composition balances ink/grunge and isometric geometry as visual influences, not as a pixel quota. Use the layered source artwork as actual assets and adapt the black-and-white HUD material in Design Content for functional chrome. Keep buttons, tabs, search, terminals, galleries, and editors as semantic interactive controls over the art.

## Product behavior

- The command center opens Home with actual local catalog and Brain data; its tabs and omnibox use the Chromium host where that runtime is launched. Direct-file mode remains a clearly labeled recovery path.
- The eight top-level pages, contextual navigation, tool catalogs, browser tabs, isolated websites, AI states, telemetry, runbooks, and project paths retain their real behavior and truthful status labels.
- Gateway, Design Studio, add-on workbench, Brain, launcher-facing UI, and other designed USB surfaces adopt the same white/crimson/black system as they are changed. A source pack or prototype alone does not count as a live implementation.
- Reading surfaces need contrast and visible focus. The Brain and media previews may use dark stages. Motion respects reduced-motion; all primary actions remain usable without animation.
- Never invent a measurement, connection, verification, browser capability, or model session as part of the visual change.
