# Third-party assets

The only third-party assets in this package are the font files below. They were
downloaded once during authoring and are served only from this local package at
runtime. None of the URLs in this document are runtime dependencies.

## Space Grotesk
- Upstream project: https://github.com/floriankarsten/space-grotesk
- Font source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/spacegrotesk
- File: assets/fonts/space-grotesk-variable.ttf
- License: assets/fonts/space-grotesk-LICENSE.txt (SIL Open Font License 1.1)

## IBM Plex Mono
- Upstream project: https://github.com/IBM/plex
- Font source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ibmplexmono
- File: assets/fonts/ibm-plex-mono-regular.ttf
- License: assets/fonts/ibm-plex-mono-LICENSE.txt (SIL Open Font License 1.1)

Original filenames in Google Fonts: SpaceGrotesk[wght].ttf and
IBMPlexMono-Regular.ttf. Binary contents are unmodified. Downloaded 2026-09-19.
Original copyright notices are preserved in the license files.

## Cinzel
- Font source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cinzel
- File: assets/fonts/cinzel-variable.ttf
- License: assets/fonts/cinzel-LICENSE.txt (SIL Open Font License 1.1)
- Source filename: Cinzel[wght].ttf. Binary content is unmodified.

## Generated and reference-guided artwork
The new hero uses the built-in imagegen tool, guided by two images supplied by
the user. See GENERATION-PROMPT.md. Source references were not modified or
redistributed. All SVGs are editable code-native vectors, not raster traces.
