# Motion and the portal

## The portal visual language

The portal is a technical machine activating, not a magical effect. Canonical ingredients: central singularity, concentric rings, segmented mechanical arcs, particle acceleration, radial expansion, spectral distortion, horizontal energy flare, environmental illumination, shell reveal.

## The operator transition

```
Landing → Operator identification → Target lock → Ring initialization
→ Energy acceleration → Portal expansion → Horizontal energy flare
→ Shell geometry revealed → Operator workspace active
```

The cinematic layer does not vanish and hand off to a dashboard. The flare expands, shell geometry appears beneath it, the masthead resolves, the rails appear, telemetry activates, and the workspace becomes interactive — one continuous experience.

## Motion modes

**Cinematic ≈ 3s** — first launch, showcases, deliberate operator entry. Environmental dim, ring assembly, particle acceleration, spectral displacement, strong flare, shell reveal.

**Balanced ≈ 1.0–1.3s** — the default. Targeting, abbreviated ring energy, flare, shell reveal.

**Minimal ≈ 120–200ms** — reduced motion or performance mode. Fade, short edge illumination, immediate shell transition. No spinning portal, no particle storm.

## Microinteractions

Hover lifts 1–2px or illuminates subtly. Selection activates an energy rail. Execute sends a short pulse along a control edge. Complete gives one controlled confirmation flash. Warning gives a short crimson pulse. Expand reveals mechanically. An operator change sends energy through the shell before the accent resolves.

These small behaviours produce more sophistication than constant large animation.

## Loading

Avoid generic spinners. Use a scanning line, a segmented ring, a thin progress rail, telemetry progression or restrained portal energy — and whatever the form, it must communicate actual state. A loading indicator that does not know how far along it is should say so rather than imply otherwise.

## Environment

Workspaces stay calm. Optional background layers, in order of subtlety: Void, faint radial illumination, an extremely faint emblem watermark, technical grid fragments, a localized operator bloom, sparse particle atmosphere. Grid opacity stays extremely low, and never sits at high contrast behind working text.

Phantom fog belongs to the landing page, portal transitions, hero surfaces and major modal transitions only. Never behind dense technical content.

## Sound

Optional and off unless the user permits it. Low mechanical engagement, a quiet confirmation, a subtle relay click, a subdued warning, a portal energy build. Never loud sci-fi effects, repetitive beeps or continuous ambience.
