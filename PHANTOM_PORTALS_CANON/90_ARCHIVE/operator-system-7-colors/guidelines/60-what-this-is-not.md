# What Phantom Portals is not

A design system is defined as much by its refusals. Each of these is a real failure mode for a dark technical interface, and each is close enough to be reached by accident.

**Generic cyberpunk.** Overwhelming neon. The defence is the 10–15% ceiling on operator colour and the rule that energy is edge-bound.

**Gaming HUD.** Decorative fake statistics and meaningless crosshairs. Telemetry shows real values or it is removed.

**Crypto dashboard.** Endless glowing metric cards of equal weight. The command center has a hierarchy; a wall of cards is what happens when it does not.

**Consumer SaaS.** Giant rounded rectangles and pastel gradients. The largest radius here is 3px.

**Glassmorphism demo.** Blur is a finish on one material, not the structure of the interface.

**Movie prop.** It has to be genuinely usable by a technician under pressure.

**Gothic fantasy.** Phantom and reaper motifs are identity accents, not decoration.

## Visual red flags

Glowing borders on everything · dozens of equally prominent cards · excessive gradients · giant corner radii · random red accents · random operator colours · excessive particle fields · tiny unreadable text · meaningless telemetry · overuse of ALL CAPS · decorative graphs · permanent animation · arbitrary floating elements · excessive glass · fake technical terminology · massive unused headers · inconsistent icon libraries.

## The design decision test

Before adding any visual element, ask whether it improves hierarchy, communicates state, improves navigation, reinforces Phantom identity, makes technical information clearer, or makes an important interaction more satisfying.

If none apply: remove it.
