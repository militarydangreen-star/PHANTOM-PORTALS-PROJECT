# Phantom Portals brand marks — retired 2026-10-31

The logo-role marks of the hooded Phantom and the PHANTOM PORTALS wordmark, current from canon 1.0.0 to 1.2.0. Retired when the project was renamed **Polka Portals** ([ADR-0014](../../00_START_HERE/decisions/ADR-0014-rename-polka-portals.md)) and the shield mask became the logo ([ADR-0015](../../00_START_HERE/decisions/ADR-0015-shield-logo.md)). The files are unchanged; their sidecars are folded into the table below.

The Phantom itself is **not** retired: the emblem masters and PNGs stay in `30_ASSETS/brand/phantom-hero/` as character art.
