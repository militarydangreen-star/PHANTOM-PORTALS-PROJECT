07 — Rules

Do

  • Let paper dominate; frame with matte black.
  • One crimson primary action per view.
  • Pair every chaos mark with a precision mark.
  • One hooded Phantom per surface, with clear space.
  • Label sample data SAMPLE; show real states.
  • Space Grotesk, Inter, JetBrains Mono — nothing else.

Don't

  • Crimson washes, neon glows, cyan or magenta light.
  • Art behind text, tables, terminals or controls.
  • Gold, purple, obsidian themes or operator colours.
  • Crimson text on black (2.26:1 — fails).
  • Invented telemetry or fake tool launches.
  • A second character, or the Phantom repeated.
