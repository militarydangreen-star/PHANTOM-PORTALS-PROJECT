# Retired-canon avoid-list

Polka Portals (called Phantom Portals until 2026-10-31) had earlier directions and marks that are now retired ([CANON_REGISTER](../../00_START_HERE/CANON_REGISTER.md#retired-canon-90_archive--read-only)). Models — and people — drift back to them. Append this when briefing anything about the brand.

```text
Do not use any retired Polka Portals direction: no obsidian-and-gold or "sovereign gold" (#D4AF37, #D1A64A, #D7B982); no phantom purple (#8B5CF6); no Ruby Edition gothic luxury with champagne-gold edging; no seven operator energy colours (green #39FF88, cyan #00E5FF, red-pink #FF335F, orange #FF7700, violet #A855F7, teal #14B8A6, pink #EC4899) and no operator personas or operator matrix; no Phantom Line with gold nodes; no PHANTØM slashed-O wordmark; no Cinzel, Montserrat, IBM Plex Mono or Bahnschrift; no alpine landscape hero; no holographic brain as the default homepage hero; no orange/sapphire/violet scheme; no v1 flat low-poly Phantom; no "PHANTOM PORTALS" name or wordmark (the project is Polka Portals); no hooded reaper used as a logo, monogram or app icon (the logo is the shield mask; the Phantom appears only as character art); no Cyberpunk cockpit — no obsidian or carbon-fibre surfaces, smoked glass, dark alloy, cyan or magenta emissive glow, amber HUD, platinum-on-black text, HUD chrome on every button, or neural brain in a central projection bay.
```
